## The project
I developed a connected trashcan for Earth Fokus during my product development internship and senior-year project. It was designed to help reduce food waste in corporate offices.

## What I worked on

My work covered the physical product and its connected electronics, from market research and sensor calibration to printed circuit board (PCB) design. I also implemented the complete IoT system architecture.

## What I can share

The short video is **a sample of the prototype's lighting only**. It does not show the full system in operation.Further operational details are covered by a nondisclosure agreement (NDA). All rights to the prototype belong to **Earth Fokus**.
