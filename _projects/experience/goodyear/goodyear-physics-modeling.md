## Reducing physical iterations

In my current role at Goodyear, I combine physics-based modeling, including finite element analysis (FEA) and mathematical modeling, with machine learning to develop virtual models of manufacturing processes. My work covers different stages of manufacturing, from extrusion to curing, with the goal of reducing physical iterations and improving manufacturing capability.
