## Managing the full product lifecycle

At MRF Tyres, I managed truck and light-truck radial tire projects and their performance across the product lifecycle. My scope included:

- Application and product requirements.
- Design and development.
- Testing and performance evaluation.
- Homologation.
- Industrialization.
- Ongoing product improvements and end of life.

## Coordinating OEM and manufacturing requirements

I worked with automotive manufacturers (OEMs), manufacturing plants, and other stakeholders to develop and manage tires for their applications. This connected customer requirements with the engineering work needed to develop, test, and manufacture the product.

## Managing performance, cost, and product issues

As products progressed through their lifecycle, I reviewed test results and performance in use and worked through reported issues. I considered product performance and cost when working on design and manufacturing changes, and collaborated with manufacturing plants on production-yield improvements.
