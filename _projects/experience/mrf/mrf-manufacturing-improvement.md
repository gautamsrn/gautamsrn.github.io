## Manufacturing yield improvement initiatives

Alongside tire development at MRF Tyres, I worked on new product industrialization and to improve production yield. This work connected product engineering with the manufacturing processes. This involved working with multiple manufacturing plants and stakeholders across product segments and organizations within the company to achieve the required goals.

## Resolving quality issues through design and process changes

I worked with manufacturing plants to improve product quality and address them through changes to tire designs and manufacturing processes. My focus was on improving product quality and production yield while considering the effects of those changes on tire performance and cost.
