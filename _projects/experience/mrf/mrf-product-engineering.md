## Designing for vehicle and application requirements

At MRF Tyres, I designed and developed truck and light-truck radial tires for their vehicle applications. I worked with automotive manufacturers (OEMs) to understand their requirements and carried that understanding through design, development, testing, and product improvements informed by performance in use.

## Evaluating tire performance

I used physical testing and performance evaluation to assess how tires behaved and where they needed improvement. The performance areas I worked on included:

- Noise, vibration, and harshness (NVH).
- Rolling resistance and fuel efficiency.
- Traction.
- Mileage and durability.
- Ride and handling.

I considered these requirements alongside cost when developing and improving products.

## Investigating product issues and failures

I investigated reported product issues and failures, using test results and feedback from use to understand what needed to change. I worked on tire design and manufacturing changes to address those issues and improve the product.

## Combining modeling with physical testing

I used physics-based modeling through finite element analysis (FEA), along with machine learning, physical testing, and engineering optimization, to support tire development and product improvements.
