## Burner design and CNC manufacturing

During my industrial project internship at Preethi Kitchen Appliances in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, I designed a novel, highly efficient stove-top LPG gas burner. The design was CNC manufactured.

## Experimental testing and design improvements

I conducted experimental testing to identify prominent heat loss areas, which was then used as the basis for the new designs.
