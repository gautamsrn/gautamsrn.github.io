## The design
We designed a wheelchair that converts into a stretcher. The aim was to let the user convert it manually, without external assistance. The project was completed within a $100 budget.

## What I worked on
I designed and fabricated the prototype and carried out static structural simulations in ANSYS. I also did kinematic studies to refine the design.
