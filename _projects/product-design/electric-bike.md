Our team represented SSN College of Engineering at the Asian Electric Bike Challenge. We designed and built an electric motorcycle for the competition.

## My part
I worked with the team on vehicle design, manufacturing, assembly, and testing.

## How we did
We won the **Best Acceleration** and **Best for Terrain** awards.
