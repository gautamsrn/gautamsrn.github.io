## From a planned route to robot motion

We implemented and tested multiple path-planning algorithms in simulation, then executed the planned routes on a physical TurtleBot Waffle. These included graph-based algorithms such as A* and sampling-based tree algorithms such as RRT. This demo showcases the A* implementation, showing the planned path in simulation alongside the robot’s motion in the real world.
