## Following the paper path

I worked on a TurtleBot that follows a route laid out with A4 sheets of paper. Along the way, it needed to recognize stop signs and detect obstacles, then stop in response.

## From simulation to a TurtleBot

I worked on the path-following and perception behavior in simulation, then implemented the project on a physical TurtleBot.
