## Design and simulation

Designed a mobile rover with a multipurpose robotic arm, using CAD and kinematics for the design and Python for the software. The rover and arm was simulated and tested the in ROS2 and Gazebo. Future work for this project involves ability to keep different end effectors at the back of the rover and a system to exchange it as required automatically.
