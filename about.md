      <h2>Hi, I'm Gautam.</h2>
      <p>I work at the intersection of mechanical engineering, software, robotics, data, and AI systems. I start by understanding what needs to improve, who it affects, and the constraints involved. That might mean improving a product, modeling a manufacturing process, or helping someone make a better-informed decision.</p>
      <p>At Goodyear, I combine physics-based modeling, including finite element analysis (FEA) and mathematical modeling, with machine learning and deep learning to develop virtual models of manufacturing processes. My work covers different stages of manufacturing, from extrusion to curing, with the goal of reducing physical iterations and improving manufacturing capability. During my 2024 internship there, I worked on Machine Learning, Statistics and Optimization.</p>
      <p>At MRF Tyres, I managed the development and performance of truck and light-truck radial tires throughout the product lifecycle—from market and OEM requirements through design, testing, homologation, industrialization, till end of life. I collaborated with automotive OEMs and manufacturing plants to resolve product issues while balancing perfomance requirements including noise, vibration, and harshness (NVH), ride and handling, mileage, fuel efficiency, and cost. I also contributed to production-yield improvement initiatives.</p>
      <p>My experience also includes additive manufacturing at Terrapin Works, working on  digital factory initiatives at Ford, and connected product development at Earth Fokus.</p>
      <p>I hold a Master of Engineering in Robotics from the University of Maryland and a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering from Anna University.</p>
      <p>I'm also exploring agentic AI: systems that coordinate tools and tasks. I'm interested in how they can support technical and business workflows, how to evaluate their outputs, and where human judgment matters.</p>
      <p>I use this site to share my projects and think through technical and business ideas as I learn.</p>
